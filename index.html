04 / An engineering approach

Start with the problem.
Build with purpose.

A clear scope, a testable solution and a handover people can use. Each stage gives the next one a stronger foundation.

01 / UNDERSTAND

Make the challenge clear.

Discuss the process, the people and the current constraints. Agree what a useful result looks like before selecting the technology.

02 / ENGINEER

Develop. Test. Refine.

Work from an agreed scope. Prototype the uncertain parts, make assumptions visible and evaluate the solution against clear requirements.

03 / ENABLE

Make the work usable.

Provide the documentation, context and knowledge transfer needed for a confident handover and a sensible next step.
