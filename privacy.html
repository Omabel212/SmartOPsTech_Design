Using the enquiry form

The enquiry form builds an email draft in your browser. It does not submit your details to a SmartOpsTech database. You choose whether to copy the text or open your email app, and you send the email yourself.

Email correspondence

If you send an enquiry to uzoeshijuliet@gmail.com, its contents are received through the email service used for that address and can be used to respond to you. Avoid including sensitive plant credentials, passwords or confidential documents in an initial enquiry.

Website delivery

This website is hosted on Vercel. The hosting provider may process technical information needed to deliver and secure the site, such as request and network information. This version of the site does not add analytics trackers or advertising cookies.

External links

GitHub, LinkedIn and ResearchGate links open external services with their own privacy practices. The website’s interactive process demo uses example data in your browser and does not connect to a live industrial system.

Questions
